# PP-501 Parcel Weight and Service

## Story requirements

-   Parcel weight must be from **0.1 kg to 30 kg inclusive**.
-   Available services: **Standard, Express, SameDay**.
-   SameDay is available only for **Cebu City, Mandaue, and Lapu-Lapu**.
-   Express is not offered for parcels **over 20 kg**.
-   Base fees:
    -   Standard: **PHP 120**
    -   Express: **PHP 220**
    -   SameDay: **PHP 350**
-   Parcels weighing more than 10 kg incur a surcharge of **PHP 15 per
    started kilogram above 10 kg**.

**Why columns are collapsed:** The table groups combinations when a
condition does not change the outcome. City does not affect Standard or
Express eligibility under the stated requirements, so those city
combinations are combined. For invalid overall weight, the request is
